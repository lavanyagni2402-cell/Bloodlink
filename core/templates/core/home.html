How a request gets filled

  1. A family posts a request with the blood group, units, hospital and how urgent it is.
  2. BloodLink matches donors who are compatible, available and medically eligible today.
  3. Donors pledge with one tap and see the family's number to coordinate.
  4. The family marks who donated, which resets that donor's 90-day clock.
